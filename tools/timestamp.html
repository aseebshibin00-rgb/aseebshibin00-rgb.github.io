Unix Timestamp Converter

Convert between Unix timestamps and readable dates without sending your data to a server.

What is a Unix timestamp?

A Unix timestamp represents a point in time as the number of seconds since 1970-01-01 00:00:00 UTC. It is widely used in software systems and APIs.

How to use the converter

Enter a timestamp to convert it to a date, or select a date and convert it to seconds.
